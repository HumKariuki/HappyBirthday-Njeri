My Dear Ann

Sometimes I honestly wonder how one person can bring so many different kinds of happiness into my life.

With you, even the simplest weekends somehow become my favourite moments. The games, the jokes, the silly things we do, making fun of each other, and those moments where we laugh until we can barely breathe — I genuinely enjoy every bit of it.
Being with you never feels boring. Somehow, we always find something to laugh about, something to talk about, or some little memory to create. Those ordinary moments with you have become some of my most treasured ones.

And then there is your food... honestly, Ann, you are gifted. ❤️
Everything you make has that special taste that I somehow cannot reproduce. Sometimes I try to make the exact same food the way you do, following what I think you did, and somehow mine still doesn't taste like yours. Maybe you have a secret ingredient that you refuse to tell me.

And of course, I cannot talk about you without mentioning your beauty. You are beautiful in a way that still catches my attention even after all this time. Your smile, your eyes, your beautiful curves — yes, even that big nyash I love teasing you about ❤️ — somehow you manage to look amazing without even trying.

But beyond all of that, it is the way you care that gets me. The warmth in your hugs, the sweetness of your kisses, the way you make me feel loved and wanted, and simply having you close to me... those are the moments I never take for granted.

I love that with you I can be serious one minute and completely silly the next. I love that we can laugh together, eat together, play together, tease each other, and still find our way back to those quiet moments where nothing else seems to matter.

You are beautiful, caring, funny, talented, affectionate, and wonderfully yourself. And honestly, I wouldn't trade the memories we have created together for anything.

❤️ You are not just someone I love, Ann. You are someone I genuinely enjoy doing life with — and that means more to me than I can put into words. ❤️
